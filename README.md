# Signature W

This mod was originally designed by 2 fans of Supreme Commander 2. However, we aren't fans of the entire game — we are specifically fans of its heavy artillery and long-range combat. That is exactly what inspired us to create this mod: the idea that you can sit behind a massive, impenetrable defense while still maintaining constant pressure on your opponent.

We will try our best to combine elements from both games.

## Stage 1
Tiers 0, 1, and 2 of ore processing means development and thriving using the mod's resources while defending from any of the vanilla threats.

## Stage 2
Tiers 3 and 4 will add monstrous turrets that will allow you to assault a rival's base from your own without even moving outside of your base walls.

Each faction will have 4 tiers of ore processing (excluding raw ores).

## Summary
Future PvP must be long, harsh, and thrive on high defense.
